Scientific foundation

Built on Earth Observation and Scientific Data

The platform is designed around real scientific sources, with clear distinctions between visual satellite layers, catalog metadata, and numerical environmental data.

NASA POWER Numerical data

Point-based meteorological and solar parameters used for climate indicators, agricultural calculations, and analysis-ready time series.

NASA Earthdata / CMR Catalog metadata

Collection and granule discovery through the Common Metadata Repository; results describe available data products and links.

NASA GIBS Visual layers

Satellite imagery rendered as map tiles for visual context such as true color, vegetation indices, land surface temperature, and soil moisture.

FAOSTAT Official statistics

Food and Agriculture Organization statistical data for crop production, yield, area, and related agricultural indicators.

Honest data boundaries: GIBS layers provide visual satellite context rather than raw analysis-ready measurements. CMR responses are catalog metadata and download links, not proof that every dataset has been downloaded or processed. Numerical climate and agricultural outputs are limited to the services and parameters actually implemented.
